UX designer with a Figma prototype

Test a Figma prototype before the review

The problem
Testing tools cost $49 to $199 a month or start with a sales call. Figma’s share link shows the prototype but not what people did with it.
With Aero
Paste the Figma link, send the test link, and read each session’s summary and friction points.

ExamplePriya pastes her checkout prototype at 4pm, sends the link to five colleagues, and reads three summaries before tomorrow’s review.
