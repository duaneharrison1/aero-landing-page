One tester panel.

In a one-tab test the tester saw two panels, or none in the frame and a plain card instead. The fix is one panel, drawn on the test page above the frame, so it is always there. This board shows every state of it, and the places where our designs disagree, with a pick for each. All names and tasks are made up (Example).

01

What went wrong

Two panels on a previewFix
The site's overlay says hello once while the page loads. The test page started its three-second timer only when the frame finished loading, which is after that hello. So it decided the overlay was missing and showed its own card on top of the real one.
No panel in the frame on a real testFix
The overlay is added to the site's page once. It does not survive a site that rewrites its own body after loading, a page with its own content security policy, or a page with no head tag (each reproduced). Any one of them leaves only the card. The panel now lives on our page, so none of that matters.
