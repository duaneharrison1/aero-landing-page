One pattern for the site page and the test page.

A site and its test now share most of their settings, so they should look and behave the same. This board proposes one page skeleton (work on the left, a Settings rail on the right), a preview on the test page, a clear home for every update action, an honest Figma convert path, plans shown in place instead of banners, and a Visits section built only from the data the app has.

All names, addresses and numbers are Example data. Nothing here is built; every mockup uses the v3 kit. Proposed marks work that does not exist yet. The recommended approach carries a black Recommended label.

01

One settings pattern for sites and tests

Both pages become the same skeleton: the work on the left, a Settings rail on the right. Rows are hairlines, not cards, as in the website demo that Duane likes.

Today the site page has cards with all-caps labels and its own Save buttons; the test’s Setup is a stack of cards with a Save button and a debounced autosave. Two vocabularies for one idea.

The row

Title and one line of help on the left, one control on the right. Hairline between rows. No card per row.

How it saves

  • Switches save at once.
  • Text fields save when you leave them and show “Saved”.
  • Only an action with side effects has a button: a new password, connecting a domain, switching source.
  • The Setup tab loses its Save button.

Order, top to bottom

  • Name
  • Link (a site’s address, a test’s link)
  • Access (password; taking new sessions)
  • Look (favicon, badge, domain) or Recording
  • Source

Locked

The same row, control at half strength, the plan named with a lock. Tapping opens the plans dialog in place.

Delete

Only in the ⋯ menu, last, red, with a confirmation. The Danger zone card goes.

02

The site page on Free, Basic and Pro

One page, one rail. What changes with the plan is the state of the rows, not the layout. Plans and limits are from PRICING-FACTS: Free has 3 test sessions and sites live 7 days; Basic adds your own aeroapp.ai address, password, favicon and no badge; Solo adds custom domain and analytics; Pro includes everything.

The problem today: on Free the page shows a banner with Upgrade now under the address, domain, password, favicon and analytics blocks, plus a badge banner. Six prompts, none of them tied to something the person is trying to do.

Phone, 390 wide

Proposed

  • The Update block (three ways in) replaces Drop a new version, Publish automatically, Update with a prompt and the Site source row.
  • Delete moves from a Danger zone card into ⋯.
  • “Refresh preview” as a quiet link under the preview (the function exists).
  • Download files in ⋯.

Copy

  • “Offline in 5 days on Free”, never “expires”.
  • Locked rows name the plan: Basic, Solo. No prices on these pages; the plans dialog keeps the regional price logic.
  • Free card: “Free sites stay live 7 days. Your files are kept.”

Engineer check

  • The 7-day clock needs one source of truth for the days left (same number in the header, the card and the list).
  • Confirm that paid sites have no expiry before the card says a plan keeps a site live. The card above does not say it.
  • Locked rows reuse AeroFeatureGate’s tier check; only the presentation changes.
  • Visits at Solo and above stays as the code has it.

The test page, with a preview

Tests are copies of a site. Their documents already carry previewUrl and previewColors from the snapshot pipeline (functions/preview/plan.js treats an HTML test, and a Figma test with a prototype link, as eligible), so the preview needs no new backend.

Duane: “The site page has a preview, tests don’t.” The Tests list already shows one. The page is the only place that doesn’t.

Phone, 390 wide

Proposed

  • Preview on Setup: AeroSitePreview at size sm, on the Test wash, captioned “Screenshot · v3 · pinned”.
  • A 52px thumbnail beside the title on every tab (B).
  • “Use the site’s latest (v4)” next to Upload to this test, with the existing confirm once sessions exist.
  • A ⋯ menu on the test page (it has none).
  • Setup autosaves; the Save button goes.

Copy

  • “What testers open”, not “Prototype source”.
  • “Screenshot”, never “live”: it is a picture, Preview as tester is the real thing.
  • Over the limit: “New sessions can't start until you delete one or move to a bigger plan.” Wording waits on Decision 8.

Engineer check

  • A test copy captures from its own address, so Upload to this test re-captures by itself. Check the capture follows the pinned version.
  • A password-gated site clears its preview; show the initial on the default wash, as decided on 25 Oct.
  • Figma tests use Figma’s public link preview, no per-user token.
  • Truth rules held: no cursor anywhere, nothing typing, the transcript is shown as arriving.

In plain words: Aero writes a prompt, you paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, that chat reads your Figma frame through the Figma connector, writes one HTML file and uploads it to Aero with a web request. Aero does nothing smart itself.

Verdict: half finished. Aero’s side works as written. The result is a plain page, not your prototype, and nobody on the team has recorded it running end to end. Label it, do not promote it.

Plans in place, not banners everywhere

Figmafolio’s upsells are good because each one sits on the exact feature: the real control greyed out, a benefit headline such as “Need to keep things confidential?”, one button that opens the plans dialog in place. Aero copied that block, but its one-page site has six gated blocks, so the pattern became a wall.

Recommendation (A): a locked row with the plan named, limits shown where they bite, and at most one card per page, only when something real is running out.

Where the one card shows up

Caveat

  • I read documentation and pricing pages, not logged-in screens. Where a product’s in-app lock or banner is not described in its docs, I do not claim it. Webflow’s help page could not be opened and is not used.

Rules for A

  • A locked row looks like a normal row, half strength, with the plan and a lock. Tapping it opens the plans dialog in place.
  • One card per page at most, only for a real clock or limit (7 days left; sessions at the limit). It disappears when the condition clears.
  • Never in the header, never on a session or review screen (reading), never twice, never a banner under every block.
  • Prices stay out of these pages. The dialog keeps the regional logic.

Engineer check

  • AeroFeatureGate keeps its tier check; the banner it renders is replaced by the row treatment.
  • Session limit comes from the same count as the sidebar meter. Do not write “a month” after counts (PRICING-FACTS).

Visits

The Visits block looks out of place because it is the old Figmafolio analytics component dropped in: a second “Analytics” title inside “Visits”, all-caps labels, a six-tile grid and its own period menu. The data behind it is good; the shape is wrong.

Rule: use only what the app has. That is a Plausible query per site, not Firestore, and not test sessions.

What the app has today (read from the code)

  • Source: Plausible, one shared account, filtered to the site’s own hostnames (its aeroapp.ai address and its custom domain). The tracking script is added to served pages by api/site.js. Nothing about visits is stored in Firestore.
  • Endpoint: /api/plausible, one call per metric, owner checked from the signed-in token.
  • Periods: today, 7 days, 30 days, this month, 12 months.
  • Numbers: visitors, visits, pageviews, views per visit, bounce rate, visit duration, each with its change against the previous period.
  • Over time: visitors, visits, pageviews, bounce rate and duration, by hour for Today, by day, by month for 12 months.
  • Lists: sources, countries, cities, devices, browsers, operating systems (visitor counts only). A pages list exists in the API; the screen dropped it.
  • Gate: Solo and Pro.
  • Not there: which tester saw what, events or goals, per-page paths in the UI, anything about test sessions. The new design shows none of these.

Decisions for Duane

Eleven choices. My pick is first in each, so a plain yes to all of them is a clean brief.

Everything above is a proposal; nothing is built. Decisions are not written into DECISIONS.md until you make them.
  1. Use one skeleton and one Settings rail on both pages, with delete moved into ⋯?Yes (recommended) or keep the two pages separate.
  2. Test preview: B, the site page’s preview at the top of Setup plus a thumbnail by the title?Or A, the small thumbnail in the rail that the website demo shows today.
  3. Tests get no custom domain or favicon rows, and the hero demo drops them?Yes (recommended: a tester never sees either) or build them for tests.
  4. Merge Drop a new version, Publish automatically, Update with a prompt and Site source into one Update block?Yes (recommended). The test gets files first; the prompt tile needs new backend work.
  5. Add Download files and Duplicate test to the ⋯ menus?Both are new. Download files is in the demo; build it or take it out of the demo.
  6. Convert with AI: label it (recommended), hide it, or finish it?Label means new copy in two files and a rename. Finishing needs a paid Figma seat to test.
  7. Upsells: locked rows plus at most one card per page?Yes (recommended) or the plan summary card (C). The Free site page drops from six banners to one card.
  8. What happens at the session limit: stop new sessions, or record and hide them until an upgrade?Open since the first UI round. The card copy depends on it.
  9. Visits: keep it at Solo and above, and show Free and Basic a plain locked skeleton with no sample numbers?Yes (recommended).
  10. Remove “Target responses” from Setup and drop the Save button in favour of autosave?Yes (recommended). It is in the code; the v3 mockups dropped it.
  11. Before the card says paid plans keep a site live, confirm that paid sites never go offline?The mockup avoids the claim. PRICING-FACTS only says Free sites stay live 7 days.
