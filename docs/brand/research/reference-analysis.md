# Aero rebrand: brand reference analysis

Researched 22 Oct 2026 from screenshots (1440px, first ~2 screens) and computed fonts in /home/claude/research/refs/. Every image was viewed.
Three captures failed: openai.com and lovable.dev show Cloudflare check pages, x.ai shows a "blocked" page. Notes on those three use only what is visible (OpenAI knot mark, Lovable gradient heart) plus general knowledge, and say so.

---

## 1. Site by site

**Linear**
- Logo: small custom symbol (a disc cut by diagonal lines) plus a plain wordmark set in their sans.
- Type: Inter Variable at weight 510 (between regular and medium), hero 64px, sentence case, tight tracking. Body text is grey (#8A8F98) on near-black (#08090A).
- Colour: near-monochrome dark. Colour shows up only as tiny status icons (yellow, orange, blue).
- Imagery: a full product UI screenshot with a soft grey light floor under it. The headline is two-tone (white phrase, then grey continuation).
- Distinctive: everything is restrained, and the in-product screenshot does the selling. The odd 510 weight reads "engineered".

**Figma**
- Logo: the five-shape multicolour symbol, with no wordmark in the nav.
- Type: figmaSans, a custom variable face. 400 at 56px for the hero, 330 for body.
- Colour: white page with black ink. Colour comes from the work shown (photo collage, app screens). The Config banner uses a wild custom display face.
- Layout: three columns (headline, stacked imagery, oversized "Get started" button), with lots of white space.
- Distinctive: a neutral frame around loud, varied creative work, and the oversized black pill CTA.

**Figma Sites**
- Same system as Figma. figmaSans 400 at 72px, black on white.
- Product UI in dark mode sits next to a top-right block of flat colour shapes (orange, cyan, red, green, indigo).
- Distinctive: bold flat shape-blocks used as a brand texture, kept separate from the UI.

**OpenAI** (blocked)
- Only the knot symbol was visible, grey on white. It is known for an all-white or all-black minimal frame and a custom sans.
- Treat as "neutral, symbol-led, typographic".

**Anthropic**
- Logo: custom spaced uppercase wordmark (ANTHROP\C) with a backslash glyph as the one gesture.
- Type: anthropicSerif 400 at about 41px in a long-sentence hero. A bold sans phrase is followed by serif continuation (mixed-face headline). Body is anthropicSans.
- Colour: warm paper (#FAF9F5) with ink #141413. Accents are muted collage colours (rust, sage, slate blue).
- Imagery: a scattered grid of small collage and print-texture tiles. Nav uses outlined pills.
- Distinctive: warmth and editorial confidence. Paper colour plus serif plus hand-made collage.

**Claude**
- Logo: an orange starburst glyph plus a serif wordmark.
- Type: anthropicSerif at weight 330 (very light), 56px. Body is serif 18px, buttons are sans.
- Colour: the same warm paper, black buttons, and orange only in the logo.
- Imagery: documentary-style photography (person at a desk buried in paper). Pricing cards use thin line-drawn icons.
- Distinctive: a light-weight serif at large size feels calm and human. A single warm accent.

**Vercel**
- Logo: a solid triangle, also used huge as the hero image.
- Type: Geist 400 at 64px with very tight tracking. Body is Geist Mono.
- Colour: #FAFAFA background, #171717 ink, zero accent colour.
- Layout: three columns with huge empty margins. Hairline UI cards appear below.
- Distinctive: confidence through emptiness. The mark alone is the hero.

**Framer**
- Logo: a custom geometric "F" folded-paper symbol.
- Type: GT Walsheim Medium 54px for the hero, Inter for body.
- Colour: pure black background, white type. The hero has a neon-blue glowing chat box.
- Imagery: a product prompt box with a glow.
- Distinctive: the mark is strong. The neon-glow prompt box is the weakest part and already reads as an AI cliché.

**Raycast**
- Logo: a red diagonal-cut symbol plus a plain wordmark.
- Type: Inter 600 at 64px, centred. Small mono text for install notes.
- Colour: #07080A black with a large field of blurred red diagonal stripes. That is one bold accent used at scale.
- Layout: the nav sits in a framed, rounded glass bar.
- Distinctive: motion-blur diagonals communicate speed without any literal speed icon.

**x.ai / Grok** (blocked)
- Nothing visible. Known for a black, ultra-minimal, monospace-flavoured, large-type style. Treat as part of the dark-minimal family.

**SpaceX**
- Logo: custom wide wordmark with the swoosh "X".
- Type: D-DIN, a condensed industrial sans, in uppercase. Bold headlines with generous tracking in the nav. Mono date eyebrow.
- Colour: pure black with white type. Colour comes only from photography (Mars).
- Extras: a live countdown widget (T-13:05:11) in the nav, and ghost outlined square buttons.
- Distinctive: live data plus industrial type reads as "mission", and nothing decorative.

**Cursor**
- Logo: a cube symbol plus an uppercase geometric wordmark.
- Type: CursorGothic 400 at only 26px for the hero (deliberately small). Body is system-ui 13px.
- Colour: warm off-white (#F7F7F4) with brown-black ink (#26251E). The orange "Build" tag is the only accent.
- Imagery: product UI windows floating over an impressionist oil-painting landscape. Logo wall in grey tiles.
- Distinctive: a painterly backdrop behind precise UI. Art next to the tool, quiet and premium.

**Stripe**
- Logo: the lowercase custom wordmark.
- Type: Söhne 300 at 48px. Headline is two-tone (navy, then slate). Hero copy runs long.
- Colour: white with navy ink, the famous multicolour ribbon gradient (orange, pink, violet) and #635BFF buttons.
- Extras: a live GDP ticker number. Bento cards with UI and gradient corners.
- Distinctive: the ribbon is fully owned by Stripe, and a light weight at large size. Bento plus gradient is now widely copied.

**Arc**
- Logo: a hand-drawn multicolour squiggle mark.
- Type: Marlin Soft SQ 700 serif-ish for display, ABC Oracle for body.
- Colour: saturated electric blue (#3B3BF5-ish) fields with grain texture, cream bands, and pastel glows.
- Extras: wavy, zigzag section edges and a scrolling press-quote ticker.
- Distinctive: personality and texture. A consumer-playful feel that also borders on the "bold cobalt" look the owner rejected.

**Notion**
- Logo: a black-and-white illustrated "N" cube.
- Type: NotionInter 600 at 96px, very tight. An inline yellow pill with a dot sits inside the headline ("Build").
- Colour: white page with black ink, blue (#0B6BE3-ish) buttons, and pastel status tags in the UI.
- Imagery: product UI plus black line-drawn character illustrations.
- Distinctive: hand-drawn doodle characters give warmth to a dense tool.

**Perplexity**
- Logo: a geometric line-drawn symbol.
- Type: pplxSans 400 at small sizes.
- Colour: an off-white app shell with deep teal-black ink (#0B2833).
- Layout: the homepage is the app itself, a prompt box with no marketing.
- Distinctive: product-as-homepage, plus the teal-tinted ink.

**Rive**
- Logo: an "R" symbol.
- Type: Orbitron 900 in uppercase (a sci-fi, gamer display face). Inter for body, mono uppercase labels.
- Colour: black with a cinematic 3D city render.
- Extras: a prompt-to-copy box with a rainbow border and a numbered carousel.
- Distinctive: cinematic 3D. Orbitron reads gaming rather than pro tool.

**Spline**
- Logo: a gradient sphere.
- Type: Geist 450 at 48px. Headline mixes white and grey, with underlined words and a selection box drawn around "3D" (the UI used as typography).
- Colour: black and greys only, no accent.
- Extras: a prompt box hero, then a large dark editor UI. Mono uppercase eyebrow.
- Distinctive: the selection-handle box around a word is a smart tool-native gesture.

**Lovable** (blocked)
- Only the logo was visible: a heart in an orange-pink-blue gradient. Known for warm gradient-glow backgrounds and a prompt hero, now common across "vibe coding" products.

**v0**
- Logo: a custom monoline "v0" wordmark.
- Type: Geist 600 at 32px. Light grey shell (oklch 0.985).
- Layout: a prompt box hero with suggestion chips, then a template gallery grid.
- Colour: none beyond the community thumbnails.
- Distinctive: pure utility, the app-as-homepage pattern again.

**Mobbin**
- Logo: a chunky black custom "M" glyph.
- Type: Saans 652 at 80px, very tight, centred. Real app icons (Airbnb, Headspace) are set inline in the headline.
- Colour: white with black ink and grey body text (#707070). Colour comes only from the app content.
- Imagery: product UI in a soft grey rounded tray.
- Distinctive: inline icons inside the headline, and the heavy custom grotesk.

**Apple**
- Logo: the symbol only.
- Type: SF Pro, semibold, around 80px for product names. Short two-word taglines.
- Colour: #F5F5F7 grey and black alternating bands, with blue pill buttons (#0071E3).
- Imagery: a hand-held product shot. Centred and stacked.
- Distinctive: ruthless brevity and product as the hero.

**Maze** (competitor)
- Logo: a bar-chart "Maze" symbol plus wordmark.
- Type: Phonic 300 at 130px. Very large, very light, tight. Body is bold sans.
- Colour: warm grey-beige (#F4F3EF-ish), black ink, lime and sky-blue pixel or halftone accents.
- Imagery: a collage of UI cards, a person video and a pixelated map. Uppercase nav.
- Distinctive: it is the most current-looking competitor. Huge light type plus pixel texture.

**Lyssna** (competitor)
- Logo: a serif wordmark plus a scribble flourish.
- Type: Grenette (a soft serif) 400 at 60px. Mint Grotesk body.
- Colour: white, teal (#0E6E6E-ish) and navy ink, on a light-blue grid-paper background.
- Imagery: a stock photo of a smiling woman with a laptop, plus floating UI chips.
- Distinctive: friendly and trustworthy, but generic.

**Userbrain** (competitor)
- Logo: a mint "U" symbol.
- Type: a serif hero mixed with Avenir Next 700. A mint highlighter swipe behind the words.
- Colour: blush pink with navy ink, mint and coral cards. Wavy section edge.
- Imagery: a thumbs-up hand pushing through a hole in the wall, plus big 1-2-3 numerals.
- Distinctive: playful, but reads around 2018.

**tiiny.host** (competitor)
- Logo: a purple lightning bolt plus a lowercase wordmark.
- Type: Lato 900 at 36px.
- Colour: white with purple-to-blue gradient buttons, a dashed purple dropzone and a hand-drawn arrow, then a navy band with pill chips.
- Distinctive: nothing. Bootstrap utility with a template feel.

**static.app** (competitor)
- Logo: a lowercase wordmark with a lightning glyph inside the "i".
- Type: Inter 800 at 63px.
- Colour: green (#27AE60) with a brush-stroke underline and asterisk, and a dashed dropzone.
- Imagery: desktop app mockups.
- Distinctive: clear utility, but heavy and dated.

**Cargo** (competitor or adjacent)
- Logo: the word "Cargo" set plainly in Diatype.
- Type: Diatype Variable 500 at 41px, grey ink at 75%. No images.
- Layout: two columns of plain text links and a vertical live clock. One green Dinamo badge.
- Distinctive: anti-marketing typographic minimalism with real design-world credibility.

## 2. What feels current vs what looks dated

**Current (seen repeatedly in the leaders)**
- **Custom or proprietary typefaces at unusual variable weights.** Linear uses 510, Claude 330, Mobbin 652, Spline 450, and Figma body 330. Brands tune weight like a colour. Stock Inter at 700 is what everyone else uses.
- **Light or regular display weights at very large sizes.** Maze 300 at 130px, Stripe 300, Claude serif 330, Vercel 400 with tight tracking. Heavy 800 to 900 is now the mark of the utility tier (static.app, tiiny).
- **Warm off-white paper instead of pure white.** Anthropic and Claude #FAF9F5, Cursor #F7F7F4, Maze beige. Ink is near-black and slightly warm (#141413, #26251E), never #000.
- **Two-tone headlines.** A strong phrase, then a grey continuation (Linear, Stripe, Spline, Vercel, Cursor).
- **Real product UI as the hero**, often set over art. Cursor over an oil painting, Linear over a light floor, Figma Sites next to colour blocks.
- **Monochrome systems with one accent used tiny or used huge.** Claude orange in the logo only. Raycast red as a full field.
- **Live data as a brand device.** SpaceX countdown, Stripe GDP ticker, Cargo clock.
- **Tool-native typographic gestures.** Spline's selection box around a word, Mobbin's inline icons, Notion's inline status pill.
- **Mono uppercase eyebrows and labels** (Rive, Spline, Linear, SpaceX).

**Dated or "AI-generated" (as observed)**
- Purple-to-blue gradient buttons (tiiny) and gradient-glow logos (Lovable, Spline sphere).
- Neon glowing prompt boxes (Framer's blue glow, Rive's rainbow border). This is the 2025-26 AI default.
- The centred prompt box with suggestion chips as hero. v0, Spline, Perplexity and Framer all use it, so it no longer differentiates.
- "Teams and agents" in every headline: Linear, Notion, Mobbin and Spline all say it. Avoid the copy cliché.
- Bento card grids with gradient corners (Stripe's own is fine, but the copies look templated).
- Dashed upload dropzones, brush-stroke underlines, highlighter swipes and hand-drawn arrows (static, tiiny, Userbrain).
- Stock "smiling person with laptop" photos (Lyssna), and gimmick photos (Userbrain's thumbs-up).
- Grid-paper backgrounds (Lyssna). They read as wireframe, which the owner already rejected.
- Sci-fi display faces like Orbitron (Rive), which read as gaming.
- Glass nav bars (Raycast). Fine in one place, dated when used everywhere.

## 3. Where competitors sit, and the gap

| Competitor | Visual position |
|---|---|
| Maze | Researcher-premium: huge light type, beige, pixel texture, people photos. Most modern. |
| Lyssna | Friendly-trustworthy SaaS: teal, soft serif, stock photos, grid paper. |
| Userbrain | Playful 2018: blush pink, highlighter, gimmick photo. |
| tiiny.host | Bootstrap utility: purple gradient, dropzone. |
| static.app | Utility: green, Inter 800, brush underline. |
| Cargo | Designer-art minimal: pure type, no imagery. |
| Figma Sites | Big-platform: black and white UI with colour-block shapes. |

Two observations:
- **Testing tools speak to researchers** (process, trust, people photos).
- **Hosting tools speak to uploaders** (dropzones, utility). None of them speaks to the designer as a craftsperson who wants it done fast.

**The gap Aero can own:**
- A **designer's pro tool that feels as crafted as Linear or Cursor but is light (high-key, airy)**, with publish and test shown as one quick motion.
- Nobody in the category has a crafted wordmark, a light-weight display face, or a speed-led visual idea.
- Maze is the only one close in modernity, and it is about research, not about "your prototype, live, in front of a person".
- Concretely:
  - light paper, not black
  - one warm or electric accent, not teal, purple or lime (Maze has lime, Lyssna teal, tiiny purple, static green)
  - real prototypes and real session moments as the imagery, not stock people

## 4. "Light and fast" as concrete levers

| Lever | Light and fast | Heavy and slow | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display weight | 300 to 450 at large size, tracking -2% to -4% | 800 to 900 | Maze 300/130px, Claude 330, Spline 450 vs static 800, tiiny Lato 900 |
| Body weight | 400, grey ink at 60 to 70% | Bold body | Linear #8A8F98, Mobbin #707070 |
| Background | High-key warm or neutral off-white (#F7F7F4 to #FAFAF8) | Pure #FFF (clinical) or full black | Cursor, Anthropic, Vercel #FAFAFA |
| Colour amount | Under 5% of the page in accent, one accent | Gradients, several brand colours | Claude (orange logo only), Vercel (none) |
| Colour temperature | Slightly warm neutrals plus one hot accent for energy | Cold navy and teal (corporate) | Claude, Cursor vs Lyssna, Userbrain navy |
| Whitespace | One idea per screen, wide margins | Logo walls, chips, badges stacked in the hero | Vercel, Cargo vs tiiny |
| Edges | 1px hairlines, small radii (6 to 10px) on tool UI, pills only for the main CTA | Thick borders, dashed lines, big drop shadows | Vercel cards, Stripe frame lines |
| Direction | Forward lean: oblique cuts, horizontal momentum, diagonal streaks | Static centred stacks | Raycast diagonals, Linear's diagonal mark, the SpaceX swoosh |
| Motion | 120 to 220ms, ease-out, no bounce. Things arrive rather than perform. Live numbers tick. | Long parallax, springy bounce, glow pulses | SpaceX countdown, Stripe GDP ticker, Cargo clock |
| Imagery | Real UI in context, cropped tight | Illustration scenes, stock people | Cursor, Linear, Figma Sites |
| Copy length | Two to five words in the hero | Long sentences | Apple, Vercel vs Stripe, Anthropic (fine for them, not "fast") |

## 5. Brand territories

### A. Paper Jet (warm, light, editorial pro tool)
- **References:** Claude, Anthropic, Cursor, Maze (light type).
- **Idea:** "Ship a prototype before your coffee cools." Calm, warm paper, very light type, and one hot accent that only appears where something goes live.
- **Logo:** a custom lowercase "aero" with a long forward-swept tail on the "a" or "o". The mark is that single swept stroke on its own, like a paper-plane fold line but abstract (no plane).
- **Palette:**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Paper | #F7F5F0 |
| Ink | #16150F |
| Grey | #7A776E |
| Hairline | #E4E0D6 |
| Accent ("live") | #FF5A1F |

- **Type:** Instrument Serif (display, light editorial headlines) plus Geist (UI and body), with Geist Mono for live labels.
- **Risk:** it sits close to Claude and Anthropic. A warm-paper serif could read as "AI company" rather than design tool. Keep the accent rare and the UI sharp.

### B. Slipstream (high-key, airy, motion-led)
- **References:** Raycast (diagonal speed streaks), Linear (diagonal mark), Vercel (emptiness), Stripe (light 300 weight, live ticker).
- **Idea:** speed as a visible texture. Soft, fast diagonal light streaks on a near-white page, like a long exposure of something moving.
- **Logo:** a geometric italic wordmark (about 8° oblique, built from straight cuts). The mark is three parallel slanted bars of decreasing length, a speed trace.
- **Palette:**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | #FAFAF8 |
| Ink | #0E0F12 |
| Grey | #8A8D93 |
| Streak gradient (one hue family only) | #FFD9C7 to #FF7A45 |
| Live dot | #12B76A |

- **Type:** Schibsted Grotesk or Host Grotesk (display at 400 to 500, tight) plus Geist (UI).
- **Risk:** the streaks can slide into generic "motion gradient" wallpaper or echo Raycast directly. They must be monochrome warm and used once per page.

### C. Index (Swiss typographic, designer-credible)
- **References:** Cargo, Vercel, the Spline selection box, SpaceX (live data).
- **Idea:** pure type and live data. The page reads like a well-set spec sheet: "Live in 00:41. 3 sessions. 7 friction points." Numbers do the selling.
- **Logo:** a plain but precisely drawn "Aero" wordmark with one modified glyph, a cut "e" whose bar extends forward like a progress line. The mark is that "e". UI selection handles are used as a graphic device around key words.
- **Palette:**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | #FCFCFB |
| Ink | #111111 |
| Grey | #6E6E6E |
| Hairline | #E6E6E3 |
| Single signal | #2BD96B (electric green, used only for "live") |

- **Type:** Hanken Grotesk or Familjen Grotesk (display at 400) plus JetBrains Mono or Geist Mono (data).
- **Risk:** it can feel austere or "unfinished" to non-designers, and the green is close to static.app. Could swap the signal for #FF3D2E.

### D. Afterglow Dark (the pro-dark family, done light-handed)
- **References:** Linear, SpaceX, x.ai, Spline.
- **Idea:** a premium dark instrument, the one most like what the owner already respects. Kept "light" through thin type, lots of black space and a single warm glow at the horizon.
- **Logo:** a wide, extended-width uppercase wordmark (SpaceX-like proportion but softer). The mark is a horizon arc with a small dot rising, meaning "going live". Not a plane.
- **Palette:**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | #0A0A0B |
| Surface | #141416 |
| Ink | #F2F2EF |
| Grey | #8B8B90 |
| Glow accent | #FF6B3D, used only as a gradient floor under the UI, Linear-style |

- **Type:** Archivo (Expanded width for the wordmark and headlines at 300 to 400) plus Geist (UI) and Geist Mono.
- **Risk:** this is the most crowded look in tech right now. It conflicts with "light", and on its own it would not stand out from Linear or Framer.

### E. Studio Colour (bright, crafted, designer-playful). Not the dark family.
- **References:** Figma and Figma Sites (flat colour shape-blocks), Mobbin (heavy grotesk, inline icons), Arc (personality), Notion (inline pill).
- **Idea:** Aero speaks to designers in their own language. Flat colour blocks taken from real prototypes, with confident type. Fun but pro.
- **Logo:** a rounded custom geometric wordmark with a tight, chunky "a". The mark is a single rounded "a" in a squircle, like an app icon, that changes fill colour per product area (Sites, Testing, Responsive).
- **Palette:**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | #FFFDF8 |
| Ink | #141414 |
| Coral | #FF5B3A |
| Sky | #8CC8FF |
| Butter | #FFD84D |
| Moss | #1F6B4A |

  Blocks are used only in brand moments, and the UI stays monochrome.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display at 500 to 600, optical size axis) plus DM Sans or Geist (UI).
- **Risk:** it can tip into childish or "Figma copy", and multicolour needs discipline. Avoid saturated blue fields so it does not drift toward the rejected cobalt poster.

### F. Field Light (human, documentary, testing-first). Not the dark family.
- **References:** the Claude photography, the Cursor painting-behind-UI, the Anthropic collage, the Maze people-in-UI collage.
- **Idea:** the product is about real people using your prototype, so show that moment beautifully. Documentary photo crops of hands on phones and faces mid-thought, overlaid with crisp Aero UI chips ("hesitated 4s", "tapped back"). It is warm and quick, and people come first.
- **Logo:** a lowercase wordmark in a humanist sans with a soft, open "e". The mark is a small cursor-and-dot gesture (a pointer meeting a person), drawn in one stroke.
- **Palette:**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | #F5F3EE |
| Ink | #1A1915 |
| Grey | #77736A |
| Accent | #E8552B (warm vermilion) |
| Friction marker | #FFC53D (UI tags only) |

- **Type:** Fraunces at a light optical size (300 to 400) or Newsreader for display, plus Onest or Geist for UI.
- **Risk:** it depends on good, consistent photography, which is costly for a solo founder. It could look like Lyssna or Maze if the photos turn stock-like. It is also less "tool".

**Recommendation for the next round:** take A (Paper Jet), C (Index) and E (Studio Colour) forward as three clearly different directions, keep D as the safe dark option, and use B's streak idea as a motion device inside whichever wins.
- A and C deliver "light and fast" most directly.
- E is the boldest non-dark option.
- All three leave the competitor colour lanes (lime, teal, purple, green) alone, apart from C's green, which is easy to swap.
