Billing

Questions about paying.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free has 3 test sessions with recordings, transcripts and summaries, and your sites stay live for 7 days.

How does the free trial work?

Basic, Solo and Pro each start with a 7-day free trial. You can cancel before it ends.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are monthly, with no contract. Cancel from your account in a couple of clicks; there is no call.

What counts as a test session?

One person taking one of your tests. Today the number on your plan is a total of sessions stored, not a monthly allowance.

Are transcripts and summaries extra?

No. Every plan, Free included, transcribes and summarises each session.

Why do I see a different price at checkout?

Aero has regional prices. The site shows USD; checkout shows the price for where you are.

What happens to a free site after 7 days?

It goes offline. It keeps its address and versions, and a paid plan keeps sites live.

Do my testers pay or sign up?

No. They open your link and start. They never need an account.

Is there a team or yearly plan?

Not today. Aero is priced for one person, paid monthly. There are no seats, panels or sales calls.

What does priority support mean?

On Pro, your questions go to the front of the queue.
