Buttons and the logo in use

Exploration, not decided. Eight button families and seven logo options, each tried in the moments where Aero actually shows them. Example data throughout (Kestrel Studio, Pocket, Tester B). Glass refraction renders in Chromium; other browsers get a frosted blur.

Button families

Eight families, each as primary, secondary, small, disabled and loading, in pill and rounded rectangle. Glass families sit over real prototype content, because that is the only place glass reads.

Buttons in context

The same eight families in five real moments. Every moment has detailed content near the buttons, so glass is judged where it would live, not on flat white.

Logo options

Seven options drawn as outlines from the real fonts. Each at large size, at 24px, and as the favicon at 32px and 16px, on light and dark.

Logo in use

Each option where people will actually meet it: the app sidebar, the tester screen footer (Free plan only), a share email, the browser tab and the website nav.

Recommendation

Three pairings worth putting in front of people, from safest to boldest, each with what would settle it.
