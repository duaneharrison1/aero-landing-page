Example data · PocketTranscribed after the session

Light variant · same screen, same moment

Light is a setting, not a different layout.

Every token flips; the canvas stays a soft grey so prototypes of any colour still read. The word highlight and the amber friction marks keep their meaning in both.

Friction points are timecodes first. Clicking 03:08 in the list, the transcript or on the timeline all do the same thing: jump the recording there.

Not drawn on purpose: a cursor. Tab recordings have none today. Cursor tracking is a separate proposal (see research/cursor-recording.md).

Motion · Words light up as they are said in the recorded audio (word timings are saved with each session). The transcript scrolls to keep the current line a third of the way down. The playhead moves; markers never animate. Reduced motion: the line highlights, the words do not.
