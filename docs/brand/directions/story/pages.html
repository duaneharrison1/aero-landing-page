f1

The flow: from prompt to first session

One page that keeps a log. The test page is written entry by entry with a time, newest at the bottom, from the prompt to the first quote. No stepper, no new screens: you read your way down.

Times are an example of one Sunday. The same log is the Pocket page's history later: once the first session is in, it folds into one “History” line at the top of the Pocket page, and the sessions take its place.
