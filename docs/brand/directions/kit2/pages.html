Buttons, in the app

Four realistic button systems, each drawn full size on the same seven moments: the dashboard, a session, three modals, the tester screen and the disabled and loading states. Only the buttons change. Example data throughout.

Near-black solid primary, white secondary with a hairline. The calmest choice: the blue dot in the logo stays the only bright thing, and it reads the same on every screen. Suits Aero if the app should feel like a precise tool.

06Recommendation

Pick option 3: ink solid where you read, accessible glass only where controls float. On the dashboard and in every modal it is option 1, the calmest and most legible set (16.8:1), and the blue dot stays the one bright thing.

Glass earns its place in the three spots where Aero can’t know what is behind a control (the player over the recording, the task chip, the tester’s “I’m done” and folded pill), and the measured worst-pixel ratios above show it now holds AA there.

Keep option 2’s deep blue (#254A8D) in reserve for marketing calls to action, where a coloured primary helps. In the app it makes every screen a little louder and puts a second blue next to the dot. Drop option 4: calm, but every button becomes a soft pill, so Publish and the row actions look alike and the dashboard drifts towards the “pills on everything” look.
