  • No account for testers. Only this tab and their voice are recorded.
  • Optional tasks, in a small panel they can move out of the way.
  • Try it yourself first. “Preview as tester” is free and stays out of your results.

07 Questions

Asked often.

Do my testers need an account?

No. They open the link on their own computer and start. You choose who gets it: a client, a friend, a classmate.

What exactly is recorded?

The browser tab with your prototype and the tester’s voice as they think aloud. Not their camera and not their other tabs, so you see what they did and hear what they said.

How does the prompt publish my site?

Aero gives you one prompt with a key in it. You copy it into the Claude or ChatGPT chat where you built the thing, and it sends the files to Aero. There is nothing to install or connect.

How soon is the transcript ready?

About a minute after each session ends. It follows the recording, with a short summary and the friction points quoted in the tester’s own words.

Can I try a test myself first?

Yes. “Preview as tester” runs the test as a tester would. It is free, shows as You, and stays out of Insights.

What do I get for free?

3 test sessions with recordings included, and sites that stay live for 7 days. Paid plans start at $3 a month with a 7-day free trial, monthly, no call, no contract.
