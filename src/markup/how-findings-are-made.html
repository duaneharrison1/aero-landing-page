Review

How findings are made.

From a recording to what to fix first. Each test session is recorded and turned into a transcript with a time on every word, about a minute after a short session. Aero reads the transcript for moments where someone got stuck, got confused, asked for something or was pleased. Across sessions it groups the same moments and ranks them. Every finding links to the words and the minute it came from.

2 · Moments

Picked out, with their quotes.

A language model reads the transcript and picks out moments of four kinds. It reads the words only, and it is told to quote the tester exactly. Aero then looks for each quote in the timed transcript to find when it was said.

How a quote is placed

  • Dropped fillers such as “um” are allowed.
  • Punctuation and capital letters are ignored.
  • Numbers written as words still match.
  • Most of the quote has to match.

Quotes it cannot find

If a quote is missing from the transcript, the moment appears in the summary and stays off the timeline. A guess could send you to the wrong place.

Linked to a task

Each moment is linked to the task on screen when it was said. Words up to 15 seconds after a task ends still count, because people keep talking on the rating screen.

4 · What to keep in mind

Useful, and honest about its limits.

Findings tell you where to look. Watch the moment before you change anything.

Words only

The model reads the transcript. Someone who struggles in silence shows as a quiet stretch on the timeline and gets no finding.

One voice

Speakers are not separated. Anything the microphone picks up goes into the same transcript.

Severity is a first sort

It is a judgement from the words alone, so read the quote beside it.

Counts come from the model

The numbers beside a fix or theme are the model’s own count. Check the quotes.

A small sample

A handful of sessions shows what confuses people. It says nothing about how many of your visitors it would confuse.

Voice and one tab

Aero records the voice and one browser tab. There is no camera, and the mouse pointer is not recorded.
